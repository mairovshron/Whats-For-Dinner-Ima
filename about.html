Our story

The question every kitchen asks

"Ima, what's for dinner?" It's the question that starts every evening in our house — and the one that started this whole project.

👩‍🍳

From a Friday-night kitchen to yours

What's For Dinner, Ima? started the way most family recipes do — scribbled on index cards, tested a hundred times, and passed down because they worked. Challah that actually rises. Roast chicken that's never dry. A matzo ball soup recipe guarded like a state secret until it finally wasn't.

Every recipe here has been made for a real table — Shabbat dinners, weeknight scrambles, holiday spreads, and the nights when "whatever's in the fridge" needed to become something worth sitting down for. Nothing is published until it's been cooked, tasted, and approved by the toughest critics in the house: the kids.

Our goal is simple — take the Jewish and Israeli home cooking we grew up with, write it down in plain language with real measurements and real timing, and make it easy enough that "what's for dinner" stops being the hardest question of the day.
